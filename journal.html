Trades arrive by themselves from TradingView trade sync and broker connections, matched to the desk's view when you entered. The coloured edge on a stop, emotion or note shows where it came from: your broker the desk you. Grey italic text is a draft; ✓ accepts it. The ▾ next to a stop holds "No stop used". Your entry always wins and nothing automatic overwrites it. Emotion is only ever set by you; flags such as Revenge or FOMO suggest one for a one-click confirm. Edits to journalled trades are saved as corrections, never overwrites.

## 2. Daily pre-market brief (template)

Prepared before 15:30 EAT and saved as `Market_Research/YYYY-MM-DD.md`.

```
Date / prepared at (EAT) / data sources and timestamps

1. Scheduled events today (time EAT, tier, consensus if known)
2. Events that warrant NOT trading (and the exact no-entry windows)
3. Instruments to watch: XAUUSD (primary), DXY, US 10y yield, US100 (context)
4. Volatility read: ATR(14) H1 and D1 vs 20-day average, from the platform
5. Key levels (only from today's charts): prior day high/low, London range, weekly open
6. Relevant setups: S-001 conditions met? S-002 range valid?
7. Account status: daily floor, internal stop, remaining allowance (from the calculators)
8. Decision: TRADE / REDUCED RISK / NO TRADE, and why
```

This is market analysis, not a trade signal. A trade happens only when a strategy's written entry criteria are met.

## 3. What to monitor and why

- **USD fundamentals** (most important for gold): Fed decisions and minutes, CPI, PCE, NFP, retail sales, GDP, Treasury yields, the dollar index.
- **Real yields:** rising US real yields have historically weighed on gold; treat this as a relationship to check, not a rule.
- **Geopolitics and central bank gold buying:** event risk and longer-run demand.
- **Risk sentiment:** US100 and volatility for context on safe-haven flows.

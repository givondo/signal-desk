# Weekly Review: week ending YYYY-MM-DD

Save as `YYYY-Www.md`. Numbers come from `python 11_Performance_Analytics/tos_analytics.py dashboard`, not from memory.

## 1. Numbers (live accounts; paper and demo listed separately)
- Trades / win rate / expectancy (money and R) / profit factor
- Week P/L and largest drawdown in the week
- Rule compliance rate; P/L of trades that broke rules

## 2. Strategy performance
- By strategy and session. Any setup with negative expectancy over its last 20 trades? Suspend it and log a decision.

## 3. Prop accounts
- Each account: equity, distance to daily and maximum floors, target progress, trading days, consistency status, payout eligibility.

## 4. Personal account
- Equity, week return excluding deposits, open risk carried into next week.

## 5. Psychology
- Highest-scored emotions from `Psychology_Log.csv`; link to outcomes. Any revenge-trading or overtrading flags?
- One behaviour to repeat, one to stop.

## 6. Missed trades
- Valid setups not taken and why. Hesitation or a correct filter?

## 7. Next week
- Calendar risks (from `Economic_Calendar.md`), sessions you can attend, one process goal.

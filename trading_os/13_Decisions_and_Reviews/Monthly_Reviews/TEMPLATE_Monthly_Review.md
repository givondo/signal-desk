# Monthly Review: YYYY-MM

Save as `YYYY-MM.md`, after the monthly reconciliation is complete.

1. **Financial performance report:** copy the summary from `11_Performance_Analytics/Monthly_Reports/YYYY-MM.md`.
2. **Strategy performance report:** per strategy, n, expectancy R and its lower 95% bound, profit factor, max drawdown in R, versus backtest expectations. Status change? (HYPOTHESIS, TESTING, FORWARD, LIVE, SUSPENDED, RETIRED)
3. **Prop firm profitability report:** fees, refunds, payouts received, pending (not cash), net cash result per firm, cost per funded account.
4. **Personal capital report:** reconciled equity, TWR month and year to date, trading P/L excluding deposits, costs, withdrawals made under the profit-withdrawal rule.
5. **Risk assessment:** any limit hit; any near miss (CRITICAL status); correlation breaches; rule violations and their cost.
6. **Skills development report:** competency levels changed, with evidence.
7. **Rules check:** have any firm rules changed? Re-verify presets in `Drawdown_Calculator.py` for firms where you hold accounts.
8. **Next month's priorities:** at most three, each measurable.
9. **Decisions:** any change to limits, strategies or capital goes into `Trading_Decisions.md` with an ID.

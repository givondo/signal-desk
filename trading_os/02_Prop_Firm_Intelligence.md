# 02 Prop Firm Intelligence

Research date: 13 November 2026. Prop firms change rules and prices several times a year. Every figure below carries its source and the date it was read. Re-verify on the firm's own site on the day you buy, and record the verification date in `08_Prop_Firm_Accounts/Evaluations.csv` (`rules_verified_on`).

Evidence labels used throughout:

- **[OFFICIAL]** read on the firm's own website or terms.
- **[INDEPENDENT]** third-party tracker, regulator, news report or on-chain data.
- **[MARKETING]** a claim made by the firm about itself, not independently checked.
- **[REPORTS]** individual trader complaints or reviews. Useful as warning signs, not proof.
- **[UNVERIFIED]** sources disagree or nothing official was found.

## 1. Headline findings

1. **Topstep is closed to you.** Topstep's eligibility page lists Kenya under "Countries Ineligible to Trade": citizens cannot trade or receive payouts [OFFICIAL, help.topstep.com, page dated 4 Sep 2026]. FTMO's futures programme also lists Kenya as restricted [INDEPENDENT, fundedfast.com, read 17 Oct 2026]. Futures prop trading is therefore off the table for now, which also settles the futures question in your watchlist.
2. **The four CFD firms researched do not list Kenya as restricted.** FTMO (CFD), The5ers, FundedNext and Alpha Capital Group all leave Kenya off their published restricted lists [INDEPENDENT, fundedfast.com, read 17 Oct 2026]. A missing name is not a guarantee: confirm at checkout and KYC before paying.
3. **The firm matters less than the edge.** The Monte Carlo simulation in section 6 shows a strategy with no edge passes a 2-step challenge only 10% to 15% of the time at 0.5% risk, whichever firm you choose. With a modest edge the pass rate rises to roughly 60%. Until your journal shows positive expectancy, a challenge fee is closer to a lottery ticket than an investment.
4. **Ranking (section 5):** FTMO 83.0, The5ers 76.5, Alpha Capital Group 72.5, FundedNext 67.5, Topstep excluded. FTMO leads on legal transparency and payout history; The5ers is the best value for a first, small evaluation.

## 3. Firm profiles and counterparty risk

### FTMO
- **Legal entity:** FTMO Evaluation Global s.r.o., Czech Republic [INDEPENDENT]. Founded 2015.
- **Group strength:** FTMO closed its acquisition of the regulated broker OANDA on 1 December 2025 after final approvals in November [INDEPENDENT, Finance Magnates and FX News Group]. This gives the group access to licensed brokerage operations, which lowers counterparty risk relative to firms with no regulated affiliate. Note that FTMO's own challenge business still runs on simulated accounts.
- **Payout evidence:** longest operating record among CFD prop firms. Reviewers and Trustpilot users report same-day payouts [REPORTS].
- **Known disputes:** recurring reports of payouts denied for exceeding an informal 1% per-trade risk guideline, including a widely shared January 2026 case where slippage pushed a 0.95% risk trade to about 1.02% [REPORTS, propscorer.com, propfirm-escape-pod]. Treat 1% per trade as a hard ceiling at FTMO and assume slippage exists.
- **Contact:** 24/7 support by chat, WhatsApp, email or phone [OFFICIAL].

### The5ers
- **Legal entity:** FIVE PERCENT ONLINE LTD, Israel [INDEPENDENT]. Operating since 2016.
- **Payout evidence:** reviewers report no documented systemic payout failure and more than $43M paid [INDEPENDENT/MARKETING mix].
- **Watch points:** the 2-minute news freeze on High Stakes applies in evaluation and funded stages [OFFICIAL]; each step needs 3 profitable days of at least 0.5% [OFFICIAL]. Several programmes exist (Hyper Growth, Pro Growth, High Stakes, Bootcamp) with different daily-loss consequences, so never assume one programme's rules apply to another.
- **Leverage:** 1:100 on High Stakes [OFFICIAL]; lower on other programmes [INDEPENDENT].

### FundedNext
- **Legal entities:** GrowthNext F.Z.E., Ajman Free Zone, UAE issues the terms; payments may be processed by Incenteco Trading LTD; the simulated trading environment is operated by FundedNext Ltd (Comoros) [OFFICIAL]. Founded 2022.
- **Payout evidence:** FundedNext says cumulative payouts exceed $350M [MARKETING]; an on-chain tracker independently verified $225.9M, covering only Rise and direct wallet transfers [INDEPENDENT, Finance Magnates, Sep 2026].
- **Watch points:** the multi-jurisdiction structure (UAE terms, Comoros operator) makes disputes harder to pursue. Automation is restricted to methods "expressly authorised" and copy trading is prohibited without written consent [OFFICIAL]. Rule changes apply to future purchases only, unless needed for integrity or legal reasons [OFFICIAL]. The terms page does not confirm whether the maximum loss is static or trailing; a tracker reports static [INDEPENDENT].

### Alpha Capital Group
- **Legal entity:** Alpha Capital Group Limited, England and Wales, Companies House 13719951 [INDEPENDENT]. Founded 2021. Part of a wider group that includes Alpha Futures and the ACG Markets brokerage [INDEPENDENT].
- **Payout evidence:** mixed. Many positive reviews, alongside payout-denial reports citing "group trading" or account matching, and a complaint that the firm requested Expert Advisor source code [REPORTS, Forex Peace Army, PropFirmMatch]. A sister company, Alpha Futures, was publicly accused of voiding an approved $10K payout in July 2026 [REPORTS].
- **Watch points:** the official website currently shows conflicting versions of its terms for Alpha One [OFFICIAL, page read 13 Nov 2026]. Ask support for the binding rule set in writing before paying.

### Topstep
- **Status:** excluded. Kenyan citizens are ineligible [OFFICIAL]. Kept here only so the decision is recorded (see D-002 in `13_Decisions_and_Reviews/Trading_Decisions.md`).

## 4. Kenya-specific notes

- **Regulation:** the Capital Markets Authority licenses online forex brokers under the Capital Markets (Online Foreign Exchange Trading) Regulations, 2017 [INDEPENDENT]. Prop firms selling evaluations on simulated accounts are not CMA-licensed brokers, so CMA investor protection does not cover challenge fees or payouts. The CMA issued fresh warnings about unlicensed forex and crypto schemes in May 2026 [INDEPENDENT].
- **Paying fees:** cards are accepted by all four CFD firms; check whether your Kenyan bank card is flagged for international "trading" merchants and whether a dollar card avoids double conversion.
- **Receiving payouts:** Rise, bank transfer and stablecoins are the common routes. Stablecoin to M-Pesa via peer-to-peer exchange is fast but adds counterparty and record-keeping risk; keep every transaction hash, exchange receipt and M-Pesa message as evidence in `09_Personal_Capital`. Prefer providers licensed under Kenya's Virtual Asset Service Providers framework once licensing is in force.
- **Tax:** see `03_Personal_Capital_Management.md` section 6. Payouts are taxable income in Kenya in all likelihood; confirm treatment with a KRA-registered tax adviser before your first payout.

### Ranked recommendations

1. **FTMO 2-Step: first choice for a serious attempt once an edge is measured.** Best counterparty, clearest rules, fee refunded with the first reward. Start at $10K (€89) or $25K (€250) rather than $100K; the evaluation tests the same skill at a fraction of the cost.
2. **The5ers High Stakes: best value for a first evaluation while learning the mechanics.** A $5K account costs $35 and a $10K account $69 [INDEPENDENT, 9 Nov]. Plan around the 2-minute news freeze and the profitable-day rule.
3. **Alpha Capital Group: acceptable alternative.** Get the binding rule set in writing first, given the conflicting terms online and the payout-denial reports.
4. **FundedNext: hold.** Cheap, but the offshore structure and automation limits weigh against it until you have a reason to prefer it.
5. **Topstep: excluded** (Kenya ineligible).

The 1-step formats (FTMO 1-Step, FundedNext Stellar 1-Step) look faster but carry tighter daily limits (3%), trailing or tighter maximum losses and non-refundable fees. Not recommended until your journal shows a daily loss distribution that fits inside 3%.

## 6. Economics: what a challenge is worth

`06_Risk_Management/Drawdown_Calculator.py` includes a Monte Carlo simulator. The results below use **illustrative** strategy profiles, not your statistics, with 2 trades a day, 1R losses and 4,000 simulated attempts each. "Timeout" means still running after 120 trading days (about six months of effort). The model counts closed trades only, so real pass rates will be lower.

| Programme | Risk per trade | No edge (40% win, 1.5R) | Modest edge (45%, 1.5R) | Solid edge (50%, 1.5R) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FTMO 2-Step | 0.5% | 10% pass, 59% timeout | 58% pass, 38% timeout | 96% pass |
| FTMO 2-Step | 1.0% | 29% pass | 72% pass | 93% pass |
| FundedNext Stellar 2-Step | 0.5% | 15% pass | 67% pass | 97% pass |
| The5ers High Stakes / Alpha Pro 10% | 0.5% | 10% pass | 58% pass | 96% pass |
| FTMO 1-Step | 0.5% | 27% pass | 79% pass | 98% pass |

Two lessons:

- **Raising risk helps a strategy with no edge pass more often (29% vs 10%) but it then bleeds out in the funded stage.** That is the trap behind most failed prop careers. Higher risk is not a fix for missing edge.
- **Cost per funded account = fee ÷ pass probability.** At €540 and a 10% pass rate, each funded account costs about €5,400 in expected fees. At 58% it costs about €930. Measure your edge first; it is the cheapest way to cut that number.

Run it on your own numbers once the journal has 30+ trades:

```
python 06_Risk_Management/Drawdown_Calculator.py --list
python -c "import sys; sys.path.insert(0,'06_Risk_Management'); import Drawdown_Calculator as d; print(d.simulate_challenge(d.PRESETS['FTMO_2STEP'], win_rate=0.45, avg_win_r=1.5, avg_loss_r=1.0, risk_pct=0.5, trades_per_day=2))"
```

## 7. Before buying any evaluation: checklist

1. Kenya accepted at checkout and in KYC (screenshot it).
2. Current rules page read and saved to `10_Market_Intelligence/Market_Research/` as PDF with the date.
3. Daily-loss reference point confirmed (balance or equity, reset time in EAT).
4. Prohibited practices read, especially news, EAs, copy trading, hedging across accounts and per-trade risk guidance.
5. Payout method tested for Kenya (Rise, bank or crypto) and its fees known.
6. Refund rule understood.
7. Fee within the quarterly evaluation budget (see `03_Personal_Capital_Management.md`).
8. Strategy has passed the validation gate in `04_Trading_Strategies_and_Research.md`.
9. Record created in `Evaluations.csv` with `status = PURCHASED` the same day.

## 8. Account lifecycle

`DISCOVERY → SELECTED → PURCHASED → EVALUATION → PASSED → FUNDED → PAYOUT ELIGIBLE → PAYOUT RECEIVED → SCALED / CLOSED`

Failed evaluations stay in `Evaluations.csv` with `status = FAILED`, the fee, the date and the breach reason. They are never deleted.

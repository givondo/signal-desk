# 01 Trading Profile and Objectives

Desk timezone: Nairobi (EAT, UTC+3)
Created: 13 November 2026. Status: **baseline, awaiting discovery answers** (section 3).

This profile separates what is verified from what is assumed. Nothing about balances, results or prop history has been invented. Where a field is unknown it says so.

## 1. Profile facts

This repository is public, so verified facts about experience, balances, accounts and history are kept out of it. Keep the filled-in profile in your private copy of the Trading OS (or in the tracker's data folder, which is never committed).

## 2. Objectives (from your brief)

1. Build genuine, demonstrable trading competence with a verifiable record.
2. Use prop firms as one route to trading capital, judged on real cash economics (fees in, payouts received).
3. Grow personal trading capital progressively, without touching essential savings or Precifarm operating funds.
4. Know at any time: how much is invested, risked, earned, lost and withdrawn; which strategies and firms work; whether the edge is statistically credible; whether the career is becoming financially sustainable.

Operating principle: risk manager first, quantitative analyst second, strategist third. Capital preservation precedes capital growth.

## 3. Discovery questionnaire

Answer in any order; short answers are fine. Each answer replaces an "unknown" above and sets a number in `06_Risk_Management/risk_limits.json` or `03_Personal_Capital_Management.md`.

**Experience and record**
- Q1. How long have you traded with real money, and roughly how many live trades have you taken?
- Q2. Over your live history, are you net positive, net negative or roughly flat? Approximate figure if you know it.
- Q3. Can you export any history? (MT5: History tab, right click, Report; broker statements; TradingView paper trading.) Drop the files in `07_Trading_Journal/imports/`.
- Q4. Which brokers have you used, and are any of them CMA-licensed in Kenya?
- Q5. Have you bought any prop firm evaluations? Which firm, size, fee, outcome?
- Q6. Has any automated bot traded real money? If yes, which account and is it still running?

**Capital and constraints**
- Q7. Is your emergency fund (at least six months of essential expenses) fully funded and separate?
- Q8. How much money, in KES, can you lose in total over the next 12 months without affecting essentials, family obligations or Precifarm? This becomes the hard ceiling for trading plus evaluation fees.
- Q9. Do you currently hold a funded personal trading account? Broker, currency, balance.

**Time and style**
- Q10. Realistic hours per week for trading, journaling and study, and which weekdays.
- Q11. Can you reliably be at the screen during the overlap (about 16:00 to 20:00 EAT in the northern winter)?
- Q12. Preference: intraday only (flat before you sleep), or comfortable holding overnight?

**Psychology**
- Q13. The three mistakes that have cost you the most (for example moving stops, revenge trades, oversizing after a win).
- Q14. How do you usually react after two losses in a row?

**Goals**
- Q15. What would make the next 12 months a success? (Example: "200 journaled trades, positive expectancy proven, one funded account, first payout received.")

## 4. Working baseline until answers arrive

These defaults are deliberately conservative and will be revised once answers arrive:

- Classification: **developing trader, unverified record.**
- Live personal risk: **zero** until a strategy clears the validation gate in `04_Trading_Strategies_and_Research.md`. Demo and paper trading continue, fully journaled.
- First live risk when cleared: 0.5% per trade, 1.5% daily stop, 3% weekly stop (see `06_Risk_Management/Risk_Policy.md`).
- Instruments: XAUUSD only to start; EURUSD as an optional second once the XAUUSD process is stable.
- Prop route: one low-cost evaluation (The5ers $5K or $10K, or FTMO $10K) as a rules-and-execution exercise, only after the validation gate.
- Weekly hours assumed: 6 to 8, concentrated on overlap sessions plus one weekend review block.
